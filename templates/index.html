Live position tracker (real open positions only)

For any game where you hold a real open polymarket.us position, a live confidence reading tied to your actual side. Where ESPN publishes a real per-play win-probability feed (confirmed: NFL and MLB) it updates with every tracked play, not just whenever the score changes between polls. For NBA/NHL (no per-play feed) it falls back to this app's own score-based live estimate — coarser, but still real and still yours. CFB/CBB aren't modeled at all in this app, so a position there won't show up here.

Smart underdog bets

Underdogs (market price under 50%) whose model confidence sits well above that price — pre-game from season stats, live from the current score. Confidence shown is the model's win probability for that underdog, not a guarantee.

Locks

Favorites the model is highly confident in (80%+) that still pay a worthwhile multiplier — priced roughly $0.55-$0.90, not already bid up to a sure thing. Not a guarantee; "high confidence" isn't "certain."

Smart NFL prop bets (NFL only)

Polymarket doesn't list individual player props — every NFL market beyond the game winner is a team/game-level total (game total, each team's total points). This projects each from the same season scoring data as the rest of the model (a team's typical points scored, averaged with its opponent's typical points allowed) and flags the posted line when the model's probability diverges meaningfully from the market's price. A simplified heuristic, not a trained model — treat it as a prompt to look closer, not a bet to place blind.

Paper trading — model accuracy test

Three independent $100 simulated bankrolls, each running a different, clearly-labeled strategy against this app's own signals (underdog/lock/prop), settled against the real game result: a baseline (current thresholds, flat $5 stakes), a high-conviction variant (stricter thresholds, same flat stake), and a Kelly-sized variant (same signals as baseline, stake scaled by edge). This exists to measure both whether the model's confidence is calibrated and, over time, which approach actually holds up — not a strategy to copy yet, not advice. No real money or orders are ever involved.
